SiteCalc — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 July 2026

SiteCalc is an offline construction calculator. SiteCalc itself does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. All calculations and saved projects stay on your device. The one exception is the advertising component described below, which is operated by Google.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics SDK. The app requests no runtime permissions — no location, no camera, no storage, no contacts.

Your measurements, saved projects and settings are written only to this app's private storage on your own device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently — there is no backup and no way for us to recover them.

Advertising

SiteCalc shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google may process:

  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • app interaction and diagnostic information (for example, whether an ad was viewed or clicked).

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us — we never receive that data. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are shown a consent form the first time you open the app, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choices at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app.

When you share a cut list

Tapping Share turns your results into an image and hands it to Android's standard share sheet. You choose the app it goes to. SiteCalc does not upload the image, and does not see or record where you send it.

Rating the app

After you have saved or shared a few calculations, SiteCalc may once ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review overlay. That overlay is provided and displayed by the Google Play Store app, not by us.

Children

SiteCalc is a professional tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's Android settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting all app data or uninstalling SiteCalc removes every saved project and setting from the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
