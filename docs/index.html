SiteCalc

Offline construction & carpentry calculators with live diagrams.

Draw your stringer before you cut it. SiteCalc is a stair calculator, rafter calculator, concrete calculator and deck calculator in one Android app — and every answer comes with a to-scale diagram, in feet, inches and real fractions.

Built for the job site: no account, no runtime permissions, and it works offline in a basement with no signal. Nothing you type ever leaves your phone.

SiteCalc is free and supported by ads. There is no banner sitting over your work — just an occasional full-screen ad between calculators, and an optional short ad when you share a cut list. Skip that one and the share still works.

The calculators

  • Stairs — stringers, riser height, tread depth, total run and stair angle
  • Rafter & Roof — rafter length, plumb and seat cuts, pitch, shingle bundles
  • Concrete — slabs, footings and tube forms, in cubic yards and bags
  • Deck — decking boards, rows, joists and screws
  • Fence — posts, pickets, panels, rails and concrete per hole
  • Board Feet, Drywall, Paint, Tile & Flooring, Wall Framing
